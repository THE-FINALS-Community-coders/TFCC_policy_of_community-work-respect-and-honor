# THE FINALS community coders "policy of community work, respect and honor"
Our community policy for merged, deprecated community projects.



### Rundown
The TFCC policy of community work, respect and honour is our multi-use policy for depreciating or deprecated community projects
the use is simple, if the original owner agrees, we can archive and "revive" dead community projects by taking them as our own, and setting limits and goals. that's what this policy is for! it is made to set limitations and goals for community members to follow surrounding its revival.
